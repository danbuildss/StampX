# StampX — Landing Page Specification

## Core Principle
> "This is obvious. Why doesn't this exist already?"

NOT feature-heavy or technical. Introduces a new category.

**Conversion trick:** "Create your first Stamp in under 30 seconds" — low friction under hero.

---

## Visual Reference
**Twinkle.ai style:**
- Clean, bold typography (large headline)
- Two CTA buttons (primary filled + secondary ghost)
- Hero visual with floating UI card previews
- Social proof logos below the fold
- Light/white base with gradient accent visual in hero

## Section-by-Section Content

### HERO
**Headline:** Stamp your work. Prove your value. Get rewarded.

**Subheadline:** StampX turns your work — posts, deals, tasks, and results — into verifiable onchain proof for humans and AI agents.

**CTAs:**
- `Create Your First Stamp →` (primary)
- `View Live Stamps` (secondary)

**Social proof tag:** "Used by creators, builders & AI agents on Base" · "Proof > Claims"

---

### SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM
**Headline:** The internet runs on claims. Not proof.

Creators say they performed. Builders say they shipped. Agents say they delivered.
But there's no standard way to verify it. No proof. No trust. No fair rewards.

---

### SECTION 3 — THE SOLUTION
**Headline:** StampX turns work into proof.

Every action becomes a Stamp — a verifiable record of what was done, when, and the impact it created.
- 📊 Track real outcomes
- ✅ Verify with data
- 💰 Attach rewards
- 🔗 Share anywhere

---

### SECTION 4 — HOW IT WORKS
**Step 1 — Create a Stamp**
Paste a link, describe the work, or connect your wallet.

**Step 2 — Verify the Result**
StampX checks: Onchain activity · Social metrics · API data

**Step 3 — Unlock Value**
Stamps become: Reputation · Portfolio · Rewards

---

### SECTION 5 — BUILT FOR HUMANS & AI AGENTS
**Headline:** Work is evolving. So is proof.

👤 Humans: Creators · Builders · Growth operators
🤖 AI Agents: Trading agents · Content agents · Automation agents
